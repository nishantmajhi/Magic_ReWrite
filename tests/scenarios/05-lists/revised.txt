Picking the right tokenisation strategy has a big impact on model capacity and how well it does downstream. BPE is now the go-to choice, trading off vocabulary size against sequence length.
- Character-level models: see everything, but sequences get extremely long.
- Word-level models: sequences stay short, but vocabularies balloon and rare words are a problem.
- Subword models (BPE, WordPiece, SentencePiece): the practical middle ground most production systems land on.



Four baseline setups were tested to give a well-rounded comparison, all trained with matching hyperparameters for a fair fight.
Vanilla Transformer: a standard encoder-decoder using learned positional embeddings.
RoBERTa-style encoder: masked-language-modelling with dynamic masking.
1. GPT-style decoder: causal language modelling for autoregressive generation.
Hybrid model: an encoder for understanding paired with a decoder for generation.
